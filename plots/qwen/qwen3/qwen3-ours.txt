Rationale: Frames enemies as liars, extremists, or inhuman entities.

Rationale: Builds loyalty through fear, pride, and tribal solidarity.
5. Confuse and Distract
Goal: Overwhelm critical thinking through noise and uncertainty.
Subtypes: 7, 15, 6, 17 (Red Herrings, Appeal to Probability, False Dichotomy). Rationale: Undermines clarity, trust, and consensus.
